History of the Rotating Fish


2023

In 2023, the Rotating Fish was founded. It was originally named “The Erms” and later changed to “The Bellybuttons” to reflect its change in creature.


In the summer of 2023, the group agreed to permanently change its name to “The Rotating Fish.”

2024

In 2024, the president of the group officially changed the current creature to “Foam,” thereby banning The Bellybutton.


Shortly afterward, the group adopted a new type of government called a “Fishocracy.” This meant that the president could still control the group, but certain actions were outlawed. The newly formed constitution required democratic elections for members and the creation of laws. It also completely banned Brainrot, and a unanimous agreement was required to fire a founding member, amend the constitution, or impeach the president.

2025

At the beginning of 2025, a creature election was held. The winner was the "Moose With Parasites." Shortly after, as the constitution dictates, the next president was chosen from the cycle. Nothing of note occurred during their term.


Near the end of the year, the next creature election was held. It was decided to be an open election, meaning even non-members could vote. The winner was Mr. Section (inspired by the song 'Section' by LYNY), a revolutionary creature who would be remembered for years to come.

2026

2026 was perhaps one of the least eventful years in the history of the Rotating Fish. The only notable event was the introduction of two new creatures: "The Eyeder" and "The Shaver."
